Nyrox Labs Publisher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Nyrox Labs Publisher ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the Nyrox Labs YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel and read its statistics. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly to read the upload status, privacy setting and public counts (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly to read aggregated analytics (views, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources) for the channel's own videos.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, Drive, or any data of viewers or other channels. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated by Google and contains no personal data about viewers.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how those videos perform, so the owner can choose better topics and formats. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner's own channel.

3. Storage

The OAuth token and the downloaded statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a local file and a local SQLite database. There is no server, and nothing is uploaded to any third party other than the videos sent to YouTube itself.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, transferred or shared with anyone.

5. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the owner's computer for as long as the channel is managed with the app. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it stored.
